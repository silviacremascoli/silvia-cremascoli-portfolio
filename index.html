About me

What about me? Well, I love languages (spoken by humans or not 🤖), technology, and improving things for the better. That's why after working many years as a translator and content manager — picking up plenty of SEO tricks along the way — I decided to embark on a new journey to learn programming languages and I became a committed front-end developer. Since a translator must be particularly precise in the delivery of words and meanings into another language, I bring the same accuracy and attention to detail into my code. Over the past three years I built UI component libraries and design systems with Storybook and Twig templates, integrated into enterprise TYPO3 platforms — and I can honestly say these are the tools I enjoy the most. I love turning Figma designs into creative, pixel-perfect components that don't just look good, but are accessible to everyone (WCAG 2.1 AA) — because a beautiful interface that only some people can use is only half the job. I speak Italian, German, English, and Spanish, and bring that multilingual perspective to clear interface copy and user experiences that work across cultures. I am committed to constant learning and personal development, because monotony is just too boring for me 😉
